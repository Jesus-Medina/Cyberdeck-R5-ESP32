# 16 — Compras V0.1: Chile vs AliExpress

## Regla de sourcing

Los componentes que determinan seguridad o protección se compran preferentemente a distribuidores autorizados:

- fuente AC/DC;
- TPS2553DBVR-1;
- ADuM1201;
- EEPROM Microchip si queremos trazabilidad.

AliExpress se acepta para:

- breakout boards;
- TCA9548A;
- OLED;
- INA3221 module;
- XL4015 prototype module;
- CP2102 module;
- perfboard;
- JST;
- borneras;
- adaptadores SOT23;
- LEDs/switches.

No se considera equivalente una publicación genérica de AliExpress a un componente genuino de TI/ADI.

## Lista AliExpress

### Comprar allí sin mayor preocupación

- ESP32-S3 DevKitC-1 compatible, preferably Espressif-labeled seller with high order count;
- SH1106 OLED;
- TCA9548A breakout;
- INA3221 breakout;
- XL4015 CC/CV module;
- CP2102 USB-UART;
- SOT23-6 -> DIP adapters;
- JST-XH kits;
- screw terminal blocks;
- perfboard;
- switch packs;
- LED/resistor/capacitor kits;
- wire and heat-shrink.

### Preferir distribuidor autorizado aunque el resto del pedido sea AliExpress

- Mean Well GST60A12-P1J;
- TPS2553DBVR-1;
- ADuM1201BRZ.

Reason: these three devices protect or isolate the system. A counterfeit/substituted part undermines the reason we chose them.

### EEPROM

A generic 24C02 is fine for a throwaway V0.1 ID test. For the final carrier use genuine Microchip 24LC02B or equivalent from an authorized distributor.

## Do not buy

- open-frame mains PSU for V0.1;
- generic "5A" DC switches without a DC rating;
- unbranded protection ICs marketed only by package marking;
- USB isolator that supplies the ESP32 from the Dell side;
- lithium cells/BMS for this phase.

## Acceptance inspection

Before use:

- verify markings;
- inspect soldering;
- meter continuity;
- check no reversed polarity;
- verify 5 V rail unloaded;
- load-test regulator with dummy load;
- verify fuse actually opens;
- verify supervisor switch gives 0 V when OFF.
